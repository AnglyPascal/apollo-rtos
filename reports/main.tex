The report should be considered to be a technical document designed to be readable by a
computer scientist who is not a specialist in the topic, say one of your colleagues.
The sort of structure that would suit many programming projects is as follows:  
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Abstract: a brief description of what you did; about 200 words.  
    \item Contents.  
    \item Chapters 1, 2, etc. Areas to cover: Introduction, Explanation of the Problem
        or Context, Description of the Method, Account of the Work (several chapters,
        the main body of the dissertation), Conclusions.  
    \item A bibliography and list of reference
\end{enumerate}
